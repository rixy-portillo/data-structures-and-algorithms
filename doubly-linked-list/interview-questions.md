# Doubly Linked List Interview Questions

## Concept Questions

1. What is a doubly linked list?

2. What is the difference between a singly linked list and a doubly linked list?

3. What are the advantages of a doubly linked list?

4. What are the disadvantages of a doubly linked list?

5. Why does a doubly linked list require more memory than a singly linked list?

6. What should the previous pointer of the head contain?

7. What should the next pointer of the tail contain?

8. What happens to head and tail when the list contains only one node?

9. What is the time complexity of inserting at the head?

10. What is the time complexity of inserting at the tail when a tail pointer is stored?

11. Why can removeTail be O(1) in a doubly linked list?

12. Why is searching for a value still O(n)?
